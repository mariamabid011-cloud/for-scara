For My Platonic Soulmate

To my Scara, on your special day ♡

✿

My dearest Scara,

Happy birthday to one of the most special people in my life. ♡ I wish I could put into words just how much you mean to me, but somehow, words never seem to be enough when it comes to the people who matter the most.

I'm so grateful that life gave me you. Out of all the people in this world, I'm grateful that I get to know you, care about you, laugh with you, and call you my best friend. Sometimes I think about how lucky I am to have someone like you in my life, and I genuinely hope you know how loved and appreciated you are.

You're not just my best friend. You're my platonic soulmate. You're someone who means so much to me in a way that is completely our own. You're someone I want to see happy, someone whose dreams I want to see come true, and someone I'll always wish the very best for. Having you in my life is something I will never take for granted.

And Scara, I want you to remember something, especially on the days when life doesn't feel so kind: you will always have someone in your corner in me. No matter what you're going through, no matter how confusing things get, and no matter how far apart we are, I'll always care about you. You don't have to be happy all the time around me. You don't have to pretend that everything is okay when it isn't. You can have difficult days, make mistakes, feel lost, or simply need someone to listen. You deserve that same care and understanding on your hardest days as you do on your best ones.

I might not always have the perfect words to say, and I might not always know how to make everything better, but I'll do my best to listen, to understand, and to remind you that you matter. I'll celebrate your little victories with you, cheer you on when you're chasing your dreams, and remind you of how capable you are when you start doubting yourself. I'll be rooting for you even when you can't see how far you've come.

I hope you never feel like you have to earn my care by being useful, funny, strong, or okay. You are worth caring about simply because you're you. You don't have to prove anything to deserve kindness. And even when we can't talk as much as we'd like, or when life gets busy and everything around us changes, I will still wish good things for you. Our lives won't always look the same, but that doesn't make the friendship we share any less meaningful to me.

One of the things I wish for you most is that you learn to see yourself with the same kindness that the people who love you see you with. I hope you give yourself credit for trying. I hope you become proud of the person you're growing into. I hope you find people and places that make you feel safe enough to be yourself, and I hope you never settle for a life where you have to make yourself smaller just to be accepted.

I hope this new year of your life brings you the kind of happiness that stays, the kind of peace that lets you breathe a little easier, and the kind of love that never makes you question whether you're worthy of it. I hope your dreams get closer, your worries get lighter, and you find beautiful reasons to smile even on ordinary days. And whenever something wonderful happens to you, I hope you know that there is someone out here who will be genuinely happy for you.

I wish distance weren't a thing, because if I could, I'd want to celebrate with you in person, give you your gift, and make sure your birthday felt as special as you are to me. But even from far away, I hope this little corner of the internet can remind you that someone thought about you, put their heart into something for you, and wanted you to have something that you could keep.

Thank you for being you. Thank you for being someone I can treasure as my best friend. Thank you for the happiness you bring into my life simply by being part of it. I hope I can give you even a little of the comfort, happiness, and warmth that you deserve.

No matter where life takes us, I will always be rooting for you. I'll always hope you find happiness, reach your goals, and become someone you're proud of. And whenever you need a reminder that someone believes in you, I hope you'll remember that you have me.

So, happy birthday, my platonic soulmate. I am so, so glad that you're my person in this beautiful friendship of ours. If I could wish one thing for you today, it would be that you could see yourself through my eyes, even for a moment, and understand just how precious you are.

Here's to more birthdays, more memories, more laughter, more growing, and more years of having each other in this big, complicated world. Wherever we end up, I hope we always remember how lucky we were to find a friendship like this.

I love you so much, Scara. Platonically, endlessly, and with all the love my heart has for a best friend. I'm proud to call you my platonic soulmate, and I hope this birthday is just the beginning of a year that is gentle and beautiful to you.

With all my love, always,

Your Star ♡

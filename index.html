a letter for you

Dear Scara,

Three years.

It sounds so simple when I say it like that. Just three years. But somehow, when I think about everything that has happened between then and now, three years doesn't feel like nearly enough to explain what you mean to me.

We met in Twilight, of all places. You were just this person in chat who had such a vibe that I remember noticing you almost immediately.

And then one day, you joined VC just to hear me sing.

I don't think either of us knew that something as small as that would eventually turn into one of the most important friendships I've ever had.

Then we added each other.

And somehow, you became my best friend.

For three whole years, you've been there through everything. The stupid moments, the funny moments, the online drama, the conversations that probably made absolutely no sense to anyone else, and all those VCs where we'd laugh so hard we'd practically lose our lungs.

And honestly, I think those are some of my favorite memories.

Not because anything huge happened.

Just because you were there.

You have this way of making everything feel lighter. When I'm having a terrible day, you somehow manage to make me laugh. When I'm upset, you're there.

You've always had my back.

And I don't think I've ever properly told you how much that means to me.

You're everything I could ever want in a best friend. You're funny, comforting, caring, completely yourself, and somehow you never fail to make me happy.

I could probably write a hundred pages about everything I love and appreciate about you and still realize I forgot something.

And yes, that includes all the times I have probably needed to be told to "Calm Your Farm."

We've been through so much stupid online drama together that at this point, I think we deserve some sort of survival medal.

But if there's one thing I wish I could change about our friendship, it's the distance.

Because if you weren't so far away, I wouldn't have to celebrate your birthday through a screen.

I'd actually be able to show up.

I'd be able to bring you something wrapped up nicely, sit beside you, watch you open it, and probably embarrass you while wishing you happy birthday.

And if I could give you one thing from me, I'd give you the perfume I made for my shop.

Not because it's the biggest or most expensive gift in the world, but because I made it. And I think there's something special about giving someone something that has a little piece of your own time and effort in it.

Since I can't put it in your hands, I hope this little website can be the next best thing.

Every part of it was made thinking about you.

Three years ago, you were just someone I met online.

Now you're someone I can't imagine those three years without.

It's strange how someone can live so far away and still manage to become such a huge part of your life.

You became my best friend through a screen, and somehow, that never made the friendship feel any less real.

So on your birthday, I hope you know how loved and appreciated you are.

I hope you know that there is someone on the other side of that screen who is genuinely grateful that you joined that VC three years ago.

Because if you hadn't...

I wouldn't have you.

And I really, really don't want to imagine that version of my life.

Happy birthday, Scara.

Thank you for every laugh, every VC, every stupid conversation, every piece of drama we somehow survived, every time you made me smile when I didn't feel like smiling, and every time you had my back without even needing to be asked.

Thank you for being you.

And if three years has taught me anything, it's that some of the most important people in our lives can enter them in the most unexpected ways.

You were one of mine.
I hope you'll always be.

With all my love and friendship,
Star ♡
